# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indonesian speakers, learners, students, and writers who need fast dictionary lookup, lightweight daily vocabulary practice, and dependable access to words they have saved. Widgets are used in brief home-screen glances where meaning must be immediate.

## Product Purpose

KBBI makes Indonesian dictionary lookup quick and approachable while supporting offline-friendly reading, bookmarks, and daily learning. Success means users can recognize a widget's purpose instantly and reach the intended app destination with one tap.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, encouraging, and trustworthy. Duolingo is the reference for clear hierarchy, approachable shapes, confident color, and learning that feels inviting. KBBI should remain calmer and more authoritative than a game.

## Anti-references

Avoid sterile corporate utility, generic monochrome cards, ornamental gradients, glass effects, cramped text, ambiguous icon-only actions, and childish gamification that competes with dictionary content.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious at a glance.
- Give learning content visual priority over interface chrome.
- Use warmth and personality without weakening trust.
- Keep repeated interactions instant and predictable.
- Teach through useful empty states rather than dead ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, preserve meaning without relying on color alone, support English and Indonesian copy, keep complete widget surfaces tappable, and design compact layouts to remain legible with system text and display scaling.
